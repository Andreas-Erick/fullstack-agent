# Nexus audit — October 2026

A pass over the whole assistant for performance, robustness, UI, responsiveness, scalability and
security, with one question in mind: what happens after a few years of daily use (thousands of
conversations, memories, daily notes, stream records)? Each finding has its evidence (file:line at the
time of writing), why it matters, and a proposed fix with a rough effort (S < 1 h, M ≈ half a day,
L ≈ a day or more). Ordered by priority within each group.

**Overall:** the foundations are good: sandboxed, context-isolated windows; a narrow preload bridge;
path checks; approval gates and external-content tainting; encrypted secrets; atomic JSON writes with
backups; rotating logs; bounded caches for the newer features (stream access, inner world days and
activity). The risks are mostly about **growth**: several paths read *everything* on every turn or
every minute, which is invisible today and painful in two years.

## What is already in good shape

- **Security:** windows sandboxed with context isolation and no Node integration (`main/main.js:136-139`, `docwindows.js`, `agentwindow.js`); `app://` serves only whitelisted folders and rejects `..` after decoding (`main/main.js:85-95`); external links only http(s) (`main/main.js:697`); navigation guarded (`guardNavigation`); secrets encrypted with the OS keychain; remote pairing with one-time codes, 5 tries, hashed tokens compared in constant time and rate limits (`main/companion.js`); approval gates and tainting of external content.
- **Robustness:** atomic JSON writes with settings backup; global handlers for uncaught exceptions and rejections; renderer-crash reload; single-flight for the narrator, reflections and routines; time limits on background model calls.
- **Bounded growth already:** logs rotate (5 MB × 3); stream access times capped at 5 000; the stream reads only the last 12 months; inner-world days (30), activity (14 days) and rehearsals (50) are capped; the island bitmap has a pixel budget.
- **Responsiveness:** performance profiles, effects that pause in the background, the island cached and repainted in 5–14 ms.

## Suggested order

1 → 2 → 3 → 5 → 4 → 6 (the first five are mostly contained changes with tests; 6 needs care with the
speech and voice workers), then 7–13 as one "long-term health" pass, then the polish items.
