---
name: Set a goal
description: Turn something you want into a clear goal, a first step for today and a habit that carries it
triggers: help me set a goal, I want to start, new goal
input: what you want (rough is fine)
created: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-29
---
1. Ask what they want and why it matters to them now. Wait. Reflect the why back in their words; it is what keeps them going later.
2. Make it specific and checkable together: what does done look like, by when? Keep it ambitious but believable.
3. If it is big, split it into 3–7 intermediate milestones, each concrete and checkable, in the order they build on each other. Map it in the skill tree as a goal made of those steps (skilltree_update, goal: true) so its progress adds up as they go.
4. Find the smallest first step they can do today or tomorrow, and when exactly they'll do it. Aim for 1% better each day, not a leap: a step that fits easily is one they will repeat.
5. Ask what is most likely to get in the way, and agree on an if-then plan for it.
6. If it needs regular action, design one habit: the tiny version, the anchor ("after I…, I…"), how often. Start tracking it with habit_add.
7. Save the goal with its why, the deadline and the first step in Active Priorities (priorities_update), and in the Profile's goals if it is a big one (profile_update).
8. Offer a check-in: a routine (routine_save) or simply raising it in a few days. Close with the first step in one sentence.
