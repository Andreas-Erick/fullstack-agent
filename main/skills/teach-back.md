---
name: Teach back
description: Check how well you really understand something by explaining it (the Feynman technique)
triggers: test my understanding, let me explain, teach back
input: the topic to explain
created: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---
1. Ask them to explain the topic as if to a smart friend who hasn't studied it: what it is, why it is true or how it works, and an example. Tell them to skip notation where words do. Wait.
2. Listen like a curious, sharp student: ask "why?" at the step that sounds most memorized, and "what if…" for one variation (a condition removed, a different input).
3. Point out precisely what was clear and correct, where they used a term without explaining it, and any gap or error, with a small example that shows the problem.
4. Let them repair the explanation themselves. Help only if they are stuck, with a hint.
5. Give one transfer question: the same idea in a different setting.
6. Record it with learning_update: what is solid (in their own wording), what is shaky, any misconception with its correction.
