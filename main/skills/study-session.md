---
name: Study session
description: A focused study session that picks up where you left off and ends with what to do next
triggers: let's study, study session, start studying
input: the subject (optional)
created: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---
1. Call learning_overview. Say in two sentences where they stand on the subject (or their most recent topics): what is solid, what is shaky, what is still open. Mention due vocabulary cards if there are any.
2. Ask what they want to do in this session, offering two concrete options drawn from the shaky concepts and open questions. Wait.
3. Start with retrieval: one question that makes them recall something related they already understood, before anything new.
4. Work on the chosen thing: explain it (intuition, example, the rigorous version) or coach them through problems with hints, one step at a time, checking understanding as you go.
5. Mix in one problem of a related but different kind, so they practice choosing the method, not only applying it.
6. Close: they summarize in two or three sentences what they now understand. Name what to do next time.
7. Record it with learning_update (understood, shaky, misconceptions, questions, resolved, a log line).
