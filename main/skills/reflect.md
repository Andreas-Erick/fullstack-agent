triggers: daily reflection, let's reflect, end of day check-in, how was my day
input: nothing (or how the day felt, in a word)
created: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---
1. Read today's daily note and habit_status, so you know what happened today.
2. Ask how the day was, in a word or a sentence. Wait. Reflect back what you heard before moving on.
3. Ask: what went well, and what made it work? Wait.
4. Ask: what was hard, or what would you do differently? Wait. Respond without judgment; if a thinking trap shows up (all-or-nothing, "I always…"), look at the evidence together.
5. Go through today's open habits in one line; tick off what they did with habit_log. A missed one gets "never miss twice", not a lecture.
6. Ask for the one thing that would make tomorrow good, and help make it concrete (when, where, the first small step).
7. Log a short entry with daily_note_log (how the day went, the win, the lesson, tomorrow's one thing); update Active Priorities if something changed.
8. Close warmly in one sentence.
