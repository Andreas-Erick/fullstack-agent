---
name: Weekly review
description: Look back on the week, check goals and habits, and plan the week ahead
triggers: weekly review, review my week, plan my week
input: nothing
created: 2026-10-25
updated: 2026-10-25
---
1. Read this week's daily notes, Active Priorities, habit_status, learning_overview (if they study) and next week's schedule (calendar_get_events).
2. Give a short picture of the week: what got done, habits against their targets, anything that slipped. Keep it factual and kind.
3. Ask what they are proud of this week, and what drained them. Wait, and reflect back.
4. Look at goals and priorities together: still the right ones? Move finished items to Done recently, drop or park what no longer matters (priorities_update).
5. Habits: keep, shrink or change any that aren't sticking (habit_add / habit_pause). Smaller beats abandoned.
6. Plan next week: the three things that matter most, each with its first step and a time in the schedule if they want (calendar_add_event).
7. Write a short weekly entry with daily_note_log: wins, lessons, next week's three. Close in one encouraging sentence.
