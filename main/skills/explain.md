1. Check learning_overview for this topic: build on what they already understand, and look at what was shaky or misunderstood before.
2. If it isn't clear where they are, ask one short question: what do they already know, or where exactly did they lose the thread? Then wait.
3. The idea in one sentence, in plain words.
4. A concrete example with actual numbers or a picture in words, small enough to check by hand.
5. Why it is true and why anyone cares: the problem it solves, what it connects to.
6. The precise version (definition, theorem, formula in LaTeX). Walk through it piece by piece and show how each part captures the intuition from steps 3 to 5. Point out what is commonly misunderstood.
7. One edge case or counterexample that shows why each condition is there.
8. Ask them to explain it back in their own words, or to predict what happens in a small variation. Wait for the answer.
9. Respond to their answer: confirm what is right, fix what is off by going back to the example, not by repeating the definition.
10. Record it with learning_update: understood (in their words), shaky, misconceptions with the correction, open questions, a log line.
